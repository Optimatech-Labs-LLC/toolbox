Free tools. Use them.

Small, single-purpose tools that run in your browser. Nothing you drop in is sent anywhere, and your browser is the one enforcing that. Here is how to check.

Available now

  • Evidence hashIn your browser1.0.1

    Drop any file, any size. Get its SHA-256, SHA-1 or MD5, and a receipt to attach to your report. Chain of custody for screenshots and exports.

  • Metadata scrubberIn your browser1.0.0

    See what's hiding inside photos, PDFs and Office files, GPS, serial numbers, authors, revision history, and download a clean copy. Nothing is re-encoded.

  • Spreadsheet pseudonymizerIn your browser1.0.0

    Pick the identifying columns in a CSV. Get consistent pseudonyms keyed to a passphrase only you hold, plus a warning when the other columns still single people out.

  • RedactorIn your browser1.0.0

    Find emails, phones, card and bank numbers, Social Security numbers, IP addresses, dates, keys, organizations and labelled names in text or .docx, then redact or pseudonymize them consistently.

  • Tiny LLMIn your browser1.0.0

    Paste a paragraph, train a small transformer on it and watch it learn to write. The whole model trains in your browser. Same code as our open-source feel-smart-llm, with a gradient check built in.
